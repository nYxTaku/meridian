about

Meridian is a browser-based sailing experience on a 3D model of the real Earth. This is prototype 03: real terrain and coastlines streamed from Cesium ion, starting off Piraeus in the Saronic Gulf.

3D models: “Full Low Poly Sea & Ships Pack” (https://skfb.ly/pIT9V) by Muyaya Concept is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Scaled for use here.

Terrain and imagery: Cesium World Terrain and Bing Maps aerial imagery, streamed via Cesium ion. Imagery © Microsoft. Colours simplified for the stylized look.

Built with three.js and 3DTilesRendererJS.
