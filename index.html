about

Meridian is a browser-based sailing experience on a 3D model of the real Earth. This is prototype 02: three ships with their own handling, on open water with placeholder islands.

3D models: “Full Low Poly Sea & Ships Pack” (https://skfb.ly/pIT9V) by Muyaya Concept is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Scaled for use here.

Built with three.js.

This prototype needs WebGL, which this browser or device doesn’t provide.
